## The 3 studies we use
| Study                                  | What it found                                                                  | What it means for us                                |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bastani 2025** (*PNAS*)              | Free-for-all AI made kids **17% worse** on the real test. Hint-only AI didn't. | Our helper gives hints only.                        |
| **Kestin 2025** (*Scientific Reports*) | A teacher-built AI tutor helped students learn more than a regular class.      | Our helper asks a question back, like a good tutor. |
| **Petrov 2025** (arXiv)                | Most AIs got **under 5%** of hard proofs right.                                | The AI never grades. People do.                     |
