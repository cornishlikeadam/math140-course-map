## 📚 The 3 studies we use
| # | Study | What it found | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Bastani 2025** (*PNAS*) | Open AI made kids **17% worse** on the real test. Hints-only AI did not. | 💧 Our helper gives **hints only**. |
| **2** | **Kestin 2025** (*Scientific Reports*) | A teacher-built AI tutor helped students learn **more**. | 🌱 Our helper asks **1 question back**. |
| **3** | **Petrov 2025** (arXiv) | Most AIs got **less than 5%** of hard proofs right. | 🧑‍🌾 The AI **never grades**. People do. |
