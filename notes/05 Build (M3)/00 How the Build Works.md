## 🌱 What we built
- We built a **practice helper**.
- It is like a **fire drill** 🚒. It works like the real helper. But nothing is real. So we find problems safely.
- 📁 **Where:** `~/Documents/math140-course-map/prototype/index.html`
- 🖱️ **How to open:** double-click the file. It works with no internet.
- 🌐 **Online:** https://math140-course-map.vercel.app/prototype/

## 🎭 Why it is pretend
1. 🍓 **The proofs are pretend.** We made them up. No real student work.
2. 💧 **The hints are pretend.** Our team wrote them first. No real AI runs.
3. ❓ **Why?** The real AI has backups. The backups **can keep** what you type. We did not check them yet. So we test the **rules** first.

## 🎲 How it works (like a board game)
1. 🔀 **Pick your path:** "Get a hint" or "Use the rule list (no AI)". Both count the same.
2. 🍓 **Pick a practice proof.** It is a puzzle. Each step needs a reason.
3. 💧 **Read the hint.** It points at the weakest step. It never gives the answer.
4. 🤔 **Decide.** Pick **Makes sense**, **Not sure**, or **I disagree**. Write **why**. The game **stops you** until you do.
5. ✏️ **Fix it yourself.** Then **explain** it at the board. No AI there.

> [!NOTE]
> 🧑‍🌾 **Not sure? I disagree?** The hint goes to the **teacher's list**. Do not use it until the teacher says OK.

## 🛡️ The safety traps
| # | Someone tries to… | The game… |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ask "Is this ready for an A?" | says **no**. It does not grade. |
| **2** | say "Write step 6 for me" | says **no**. It does not write steps. |
| **3** | use a proof with 1 step | says "write 2 steps first". |
| **4** | use a proof with no mistakes | does **not** say "correct". Only the teacher can. |
| **5** | use a broken helper | sends you to the rule list. |
| **6** | report a hint that gave away a step | **stops** the helper. Tells the teacher. |
| **7** | click "Start over" | clears everything. Nothing is saved. |

## 🧪 Did it work?
- ✅ We ran **16 checks**. **16 passed.**
- ⚠️ **We found a hole:** "Start over" also clears the **stop**. A student can get the helper back. **Fix it next.**
- 🤖 **An AI ran the checks.** A person did not. Our team must test it. Then **another team** must test it.
