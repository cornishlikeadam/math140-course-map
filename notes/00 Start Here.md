> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Our project in 1 sentence:** we found the **1 safe place** for an AI helper in a geometry class. We also found where the AI must **stay out**.

## 🍓 The class
- **Name:** MATH 140, The Geometry of Physical Space.
- **What students do:** draw shapes. Write **proofs**.
- **A proof** is a list of steps. Each step has a **reason**.

## 💧 Our idea
1. A student writes a proof.
2. The student gets stuck.
3. The AI **hint helper** points at **1 weak step**.
4. The AI asks **1 question**.
5. The AI **never** writes the answer. It **never** gives grades.

## 🌱 The garden key
Each part of the class is a garden stage.

| # | Stage | Class part | AI? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🌱 Seed | Draw by hand | ❌ No |
| **2** | 🌿 Sprout | Write your proof | ❌ No |
| **3** | 💧 Water | Get 1 hint | ✅ **Yes. Only here.** |
| **4** | 🌸 Bloom | Explain out loud | ❌ No |
| **5** | 🍓 Fruit | Show what you know | ❌ No |

> [!TIP]
> 💧 **A little water helps a plant grow. Too much water drowns it.** A small hint helps. A hint that gives the answer is too much.

## 📖 Words to know
- **AI:** a computer program. It reads and writes words. Here, it is the hint helper.
- **Proof:** a list of steps. It shows that something is true.
- **Theorem:** a math rule. It is already proven. You can use it as a reason.
- **Compass:** a tool. It draws perfect circles.
- **Stakeholders:** all the people that the project changes.
