## ❓ Our big question
Can **geometry students** *(who)* **fixing proofs they wrote** *(doing what)* use **an AI that only points at the first weak step** *(with what)* **to find mistakes before the quiz** *(why)*, **while every step stays theirs and only the teacher grades** *(what stays safe)*?

## ⏱️ The 5-minute test
Anyone must answer these 2 questions in 5 minutes.
1. **Where is the AI?** Only at **Stop 3**, the hint helper.
2. **Who does it affect?** The **student**, the **teacher**, and the **partner**.

## 🌱 The 5 stops
| # | Stop | Weeks | What happens | AI? | Boss |

> [!WARNING]
> 📅 The **week numbers** come from the real syllabus in [MATH 140 Course Map - Unified](MATH%20140%20Course%20Map%20-%20Unified.md). The syllabus says the exams are **open book**. Our map says the quiz has **no computers**. **Ask the teacher** which rule is true.

## 🚫 Why AI stays out of the other stops
- 🌱 **Draw:** Drawing by hand is the skill. The AI cannot see drawings.
- 🌿 **Write:** You try first. Then the ideas are yours.
- 🌸 **Explain:** Talking shows that you learned it.
- 🍓 **Quiz:** It shows what you can do alone. Only a person grades.

## 💧 How a hint gets checked (Stop 3)
1. The AI gets **your own proof**.
2. The AI gives **1 hint**. It never gives an answer.
3. You pick **Makes sense**, **Not sure**, or **I disagree**.
4. **Not sure** or **I disagree** goes to the teacher.
5. The teacher checks it **in 2 days**. This happens **before you present**.
6. Is the hint wrong? The teacher **tells you**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 🧑‍🌾 **The teacher is responsible** if a hint is wrong.

## 👑 Bosses and sway
- The **boss** makes the decision.
- **Sway** means that someone changes the result. But they are not the boss.

| # | Stop | Boss | Has sway | If a computer did it… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 🌱 Draw | Teacher | Table buddies with shortcuts | You do not learn to draw it. |
| **2** | 🌿 Write | Student | Examples from class | The proof is not yours. |
| **3** | 💧 Hint | Teacher (unsure hints) | How the hint is worded | The computer writes your proof. |
| **4** | 🌸 Explain | Teacher | The hardest questioner | Nobody hears your thinking. |
| **5** | 🍓 Quiz | Teacher | Words in the grading guide | No person owns your grade. |
