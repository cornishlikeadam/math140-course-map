## 🌱 The 5 stops (matched to the weeks)

| Stop | Weeks | What Happens | AI Allowed? | Boss |
|------|-------|--------------|-------------|------|
| 🌱 **1. Draw** | 1–2 | Compass/straightedge constructions; design activities (name symmetry, triangulation, parabola arches, trig unit circle) | ❌ No | Teacher |
| 🌿 **2. Write** | 3–4 | First proofs: Euclidean theorems, triangle congruence, polygonal geometry proofs, triangulation proofs | ❌ No | Student |
| 💧 **3. Hint** | 3–8 | **Only AI touchpoint**: Student pastes draft proof → AI flags **one** weak step (names theorem *kind*, not exact reason) | ✅ **Yes, only here** | Teacher (for "Not sure/Disagree" hints) |
| 🌸 **4. Explain** | 5–9 | Board presentations: polygonal geometry, transformational geometry, non-Euclidean, fractals, graph theory | ❌ No | Teacher |
| 🍓 **5. Quiz** | 10 | Final exams/projects: Modern Geometry Exam, Applied Geometry Project, Fractal Activity | ❌ No | Teacher (gives all grades) |

> [!NOTE]
> 🚫 **Why AI stays out of the other stops**
> - **Draw:** Hand construction is the skill; AI can't see drawings  
> - **Write:** Must try first so ideas are yours  
> - **Explain:** Talking it through shows real learning  
> - **Quiz/Exam/Project:** Shows what you can do alone; only a person grades

## 💧 How a hint gets checked (Stop 3)

1. Student pastes **their own draft proof** (min 2 steps) → AI returns **one hint** (never an answer)
2. Student picks: **Makes sense** / **Not sure** / **I disagree**
3. Every **Not sure** or **I disagree** → Teacher reviews **within 48 hrs, before Explain (Stop 4)**
4. If hint was wrong, Teacher tells student
5. **Teacher is responsible** if a hint is wrong

## ⚠️ Risk and evidence (made-up concern)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Concern** | AI might leak a step/reason (e.g., naming "CPCTC" instead of "triangle congruence theorem") |
| **Evidence** | Petrov 2025 — LLMs often fail at rigorous proofs; **Run 1 (Oct 1)** showed borderline leak: tutor named "corresponding parts of congruent triangles" (≈CPCTC) instead of just the theorem *kind* |
| **Safeguard** | • Tutor refuses without ≥2-step draft<br>• Names theorem *kind* only (prompt: "name the kind of theorem to review, not the answer")<br>• 1 gap/reply, <120 words, ends with guiding question<br>• Every "Not sure/Disagree" hint → Teacher within 48 hrs before Explain<br>• Disclaimer on every reply: *"Check this hint against your theorem list; your instructor decides what is correct."* |
| **Non-AI Alternative** | Paper theorem checklist + office hours (available to all students) |
| **Non-Goal** | AI does **not** write proofs, grade, decide correctness, or replace teacher feedback |
