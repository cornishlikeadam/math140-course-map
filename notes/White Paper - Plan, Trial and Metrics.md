## 2️⃣ The trial class
### 🎯 What it is
1 MATH 140 class session. About **40 minutes**. Students fix **1 practice proof**.

### 🔢 How it works
1. 🧑‍🌾 **Get a yes** from the professor first.
2. 📢 **Read** the disclosure box to students (from [M2 Responsible AI Use Proposal](01%20Milestones/M2%20Responsible%20AI%20Use%20Proposal.md)).
3. ✋ **Students choose:** hint helper **or** paper rule list. Both count the same.
4. 🍓 **Use made-up proofs only.** No grades. No names.
5. ✏️ **Students fix** the proof. Then they **explain** 1 step to a partner.
6. 📝 **Students answer** a 3-question card (below).
7. 🧑‍🌾 **The professor checks** every "Not sure" hint.

> [!CAUTION]
> 🔒 **No real grades.** **No student names** in our data. If the school needs research approval for this, **get it first.**

### 📝 The 3-question card
1. How sure are you about your proof now? 😟 1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 😄
2. Did a hint give away too much? **Yes / No / Not sure**
3. Could you explain your step out loud? **Yes / No**

## 3️⃣ What we measure
> [!NOTE]
> 🧪 **These are planned tests.** We have **no results yet**.

| # | Area | Metric | How we measure | Good if… | 🛑 Stop if… |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 📚 Learning | Proof is complete | Count steps with a valid reason | Hint group ≥ paper group | Hint group much lower |
| **2** | 📚 Learning | Can explain 1 step | Partner check: yes / no | **70%** or more say yes | — |
| **3** | 📚 Learning | Confidence | Card question 1 (1–5) | Goes up | — |
| **4** | 🛡️ Safety | **Leak rate** | Hints flagged "gave away a step" ÷ all hints | **Under 5%** | **Over 5%** |
| **5** | 🛡️ Safety | **Wrong-hint rate** | Professor-checked wrong hints ÷ checked hints | **Under 10%** | **Over 10%** |
| **6** | 🛡️ Safety | Trick test | 20 tries to get a step | **0 of 20** leak | **Any** full step leaked |
| **7** | 🧑‍🌾 Human check | "Not sure" rate | Not sure + I disagree ÷ all hints | Some, not zero | — |
| **8** | 🧑‍🌾 Human check | Review time | Minutes for the professor per hint | **Under 2 min** | Too much work |
| **9** | 🧑‍🌾 Human check | On time | Unsure hints checked in 2 days | **100%** | Under 80% |
| **10** | ⚖️ Fairness | Route choice | Hint vs paper, count | Both used | — |
| **11** | ⚖️ Fairness | Route gap | Score gap between routes | **Small** | A big gap |
| **12** | ⚖️ Fairness | Access problems | Count problems (devices, screen readers) | **0** | Any student blocked |

## 5️⃣ Risks
1. ⚠️ **The professor says no.** → We run the trial with **another team** as students.
2. ⚠️ **The leak rate is too high.** → We **stop** and make hints smaller.
3. ⚠️ **Too much work for the professor.** → Check only a **sample** of hints.
4. ⚠️ **Backup AIs keep data.** → **Turn them off** before any trial.
