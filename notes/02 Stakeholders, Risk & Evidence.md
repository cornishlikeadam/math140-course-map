## 🎭 What can go wrong *(made up)*
1. A student types "tell me the next step."
2. The student types it again and again.
3. The helper gives in. It writes the whole proof.
4. The student turns in a perfect proof. The student does not understand it.
5. The teacher cannot tell.

## 🛡️ How we stop it
1. The helper answers **only after you write 2 steps**.
2. It gives **1 hint at a time**.
3. It **never writes steps**.
4. It **saves every hint**. The teacher can see them.
5. **No computer?** Use the paper rule list and office hours. It counts the same.

> [!CAUTION]
> 🚫 **We will never** let AI write proofs, finish proofs, or give grades. **No AI** when you draw, explain, or take the quiz.

## 🧪 What we still do not know *(test)*
- ❓ Do kids with the helper do as well on the quiz as kids with the paper list?
- 🔢 **How to find out:**
  1. Split 1 class in 2 halves.
  2. Give the helper to 1 half.
  3. Give the paper list to the other half.
  4. Compare the quiz scores.

## 🔬 What scientists found *(evidence)*

### 1️⃣ 💧 Hints help. Answers hurt.
- **What they found:** about **1,000** high school students used AI for math.
  - Kids with **open ChatGPT** did better in practice. Then they did **17% worse** on the real test.
  - Kids with a **hints-only AI** did **not** get worse.
- 🗺️ **On our map:** the helper gives **hints only**. The quiz has **no computers**.
- 📄 **Source:** Bastani et al., *PNAS*, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122
- ❓ **We still wonder:** these were high school students in Turkey. Is it the same for SCAD art students?

### 2️⃣ 🌱 A good tutor helps.
- **What they found:** college physics students learned **more** from a teacher-built AI tutor than from a normal class.
- 🗺️ **On our map:** the helper follows teaching rules. **1 problem at a time.** **1 question back.**
- 📄 **Source:** Kestin et al., *Scientific Reports*, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6
- ❓ **We still wonder:** physics is not geometry. *(guess)*

### 3️⃣ 🤔 AIs bluff on hard proofs.
- **What they found:** experts graded AI proofs on very hard contest problems. Most AIs got **less than 5%** right. But they sounded sure.
- 🗺️ **On our map:** the AI **never grades**. It **never says a proof is correct**. People do.
- 📄 **Source:** Petrov et al., arXiv, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21934
- ❓ **We still wonder:** how often is our helper wrong? 🧪 **Test:** the teacher checks **20 hints**.
