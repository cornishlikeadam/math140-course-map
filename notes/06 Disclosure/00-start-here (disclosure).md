## 🌸 The project in 5 lines
1. 🍓 **Client class:** MATH 140, The Geometry of Physical Space.
2. 💧 **Our idea:** a **hint-only** AI helper at **1 stop**. It points at **1 weak step**. It never writes steps or grades.
3. 🧑‍🌾 **Human check:** the student marks each hint. The **teacher** checks every unsure hint **in 2 days**.
4. 🧪 **Built:** a live map, a practice helper, a Garden Guide, and **8 explainer videos**.
5. 📏 **Tested:** **34 checks** so far. **1 hole is still open** (Start over clears a stop).
