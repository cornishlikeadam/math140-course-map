# ⚖️ 06 · Decision Note: What We Kept, What We Cut, and Why

📜 **Disclosure pages:** [0 Start](00-start-here%20%28disclosure%29.md) · [1 Intent](01-intent.md) · [2 Research](02-research-brief.md) · [3 Sources](03-source-verification-log.md) · [4 AI use + prompts](04-ai-use-log.md) · [5 Outputs](05-outputs.md) · [6 Decisions](06-decision-note.md) · [7 Disclosure](07-disclosure.md)

## ✅ What we kept
We kept the **48-hour teacher check** on unsure hints.
- **Why:** the student who needs help is the worst at spotting a wrong hint. AI often gets proofs wrong (Petrov 2025). A person must catch it **before** the student presents.

## ❌ What we rejected
We rejected the **open chat** design from SUST 720.
- **Why:** it gave **words only**. It did no number work. It could give whole answers. Open AI can **lower test scores** (Bastani 2025).

## 🧭 Steering: what changed, and why
1. **Context:** we added the **real syllabus**. The map now uses real weeks.
2. **Instruction:** we said **"8-year-old, ASD-STE100"**. All words got shorter and clearer.
3. **Direction:** we said **"hints only, teacher checks"**. The AI built the rules into the **code**, not only into a prompt.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 🧑‍🎓 **The biggest change came from a human decision:** making the **teacher responsible** for wrong hints.
