# 👥 Who Is in the Story (Stakeholder Snapshot)

These are the **5 groups** that the hint helper changes. Each group has its own box. **Edit 1 box at a time.**

---

## 1️⃣ 🧑‍🎓 The students (they use it)
- ✅ **They want:** to pass. To know why proofs work.
- ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** they use too many hints. Then they cannot start alone.
- 🌸 **The good part:** help at night. The teacher is not there at night.
- ❓ **We still wonder:** how many hints are too many?

## 2️⃣ 🧑‍🌾 The teacher (the boss)
- ✅ **They want:** fair grades. Students who think for themselves.
- ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** they grade a proof that the AI wrote.
- 🌸 **The good part:** they see every hint.
- ⏰ **Extra work:** they check every "Not sure" hint **in 2 days**.
- ❓ **We still wonder:** is there enough time?

## 3️⃣ 🐝 Partners and 🐞 tutors (people nearby)
- ✅ **They want:** fair questions. Good help sessions.
- ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** they ask about a proof that the student did not write.
- 🌸 **The good part:** tutors see where students got stuck.
- ❓ **We still wonder:** do tutors see the hint list?

## 4️⃣ 📗 People who can be left out
- 👤 **Who:** kids with **no computer** at home. Kids who use a **screen reader**. The helper cannot see drawings.
- ✅ **They want:** the same help as all other kids.
- ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** they fall behind.
- 🌸 **The good part:** the **paper rule list** keeps it fair.
- ❓ **We still wonder:** can the paper way be as fast? **Not now.** Office hours are at set times.

## 5️⃣ ⚠️ People who get hurt if it goes wrong
- 👤 **Who:** students (bad habits, cheating worries). The teacher (unfair grades).
- ⚠️ **What can go wrong:** a **wrong hint** sends a student the wrong way. AIs often get hard proofs wrong.
- 🌸 **The good part:** **hints only** protects learning.
- 🧑‍🌾 **Who is responsible:** **the teacher.** The teacher picks the tool and checks unsure hints.
- ❓ **We still wonder:** do kids say "Not sure" honestly?
