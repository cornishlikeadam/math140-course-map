## 1️⃣ Why we need it
- 💧 **What the AI does:** you write a proof. The AI points at the **first weak step**.
- 🤔 **Why:** students often cannot see where the proof breaks. The teacher is not there at night. *(guess)*
- 🚫 **What it will not do:** write, finish, check, or grade a proof.
- 🔬 **Proof that it helps:**
  1. Hints-only AI helped students. Their test scores did not drop. *(evidence: Bastani 2025)*
  2. Good AI tutors help students learn. *(evidence: Kestin 2025)*

## 2️⃣ Who does what
| Who | Does | Decides | If something is wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🤖 **AI** | Points at 1 weak step. Asks 1 question. | **Nothing** | You mark it Not sure. The teacher checks it. |
| 🧑‍🎓 **Student** | Writes 2 steps first. Marks each hint. Fixes the proof. | Every step. If the AI is used at all. | The teacher sees the hint list. |
| 🧑‍🌾 **Teacher** | Checks unsure hints. Runs the board talk. Grades. | Grades. Which hints were wrong. | School grade-appeal rules |
| 🐝 **Partner** | Asks "why?" at the board | Nothing official | The teacher runs the talk. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 🧑‍🌾 **Only a person decides:** every proof step · if a hint is wrong · if a proof works · every grade · if the AI is used at all.

## 3️⃣ Safety rules
Each rule has a **person** and a **test**.

| # | Problem | Our fix 🛠️ | Who | 🧪 Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | The AI gives away a step. | It answers after 2 steps. It gives 1 hint. It never writes steps. A "gave away a step" flag. | Team + teacher | Try **20 times** to trick it. Pass only if it never gives a step. |
| **2** | The AI gives a wrong hint. | Every Not sure hint goes to the teacher in 2 days. | Teacher | The teacher checks **20 hints**. Count the wrong ones. |
| **3** | Kids stop learning. | Hints only. A no-computer quiz. | Teacher | Compare quiz scores: AI group vs paper group. |
| **4** | Kids without computers fall behind. | A paper rule list and office hours. They count the same. | Teacher | Check that both groups get close scores. |
| **5** | Private info leaks. | No login. Only proof text is sent. Use only Space Bunny. | Team | Check each AI's privacy rules each term. |

## 4️⃣ What students read first
> [!NOTE]
> 📗 **Before you use the hint helper**
> 1. 🌱 **What it is for:** finding the weak spot in a proof **that you wrote**.
> 2. 💧 **What it does:** it points at 1 step that needs a better reason. It asks you a question. **It does not write steps.** It does not say if your proof is right.
> 3. ✏️ **What you do:** write 2 steps first. For each hint, pick **Makes sense**, **Not sure**, or **I disagree**.
> 4. 🧑‍🌾 **What your teacher does:** checks every Not sure or I disagree hint in 2 days. This is before you present. Only your teacher grades.
> 5. 🔒 **What it sees:** only the text that you paste. Do not paste your name or ID.
> 6. ⚠️ **Be careful:** AI can be wrong. It cannot see drawings. Does a hint look wrong? Say so.
> 7. 📗 **No AI?** Use the paper rule list and office hours. Your grade does not change.

## 5️⃣ Limits
**✅ The AI can:**
1. Point at the first weak step
2. Name the *kind* of rule to look up
3. Ask 1 question

**🚫 The AI cannot:**
1. Write a step
2. Give the answer
3. Say that a proof is correct
4. Grade, rank, or guess scores

**🧑‍🌾 The computer must never decide:**
1. Grades
2. If a proof is accepted
3. Cheating
4. Attendance or behavior
5. Special help (accommodations)
6. Academic standing

**🔒 It must not collect:** names, IDs, emails, grades, photos, or location.

**📗 With no AI:** use the paper list of math rules. Then take your proof to office hours or the tutoring center.
- ✅ **Same:** all students can use it. It is graded the same.
- ⚠️ **Different:** help is slower. Office hours are at set times.
