Student Integrity & Success

The 8 Essential Keys for Proof Mastery

Core rules, ethical boundaries, and tips evaluated for every student account in MATH 140.

Student Account
Ethical AI Clearance: Active (Stop 3 Only)
01

The Single Stop Boundary

AI is strictly prohibited from drawing shapes, drafting first proofs, or assigning grades. It functions exclusively at Stop 3 as a reflective mirror for your own draft.

02

Theorem Class, Never Answers

The SLM names the family of theorem you need (e.g., "a triangle congruence postulate" or "alternate interior angles"), but will never supply the step itself.

03

48-Hour Instructor Escalation

Selecting "Not sure" or "I disagree" on any hint automatically routes the interaction to Professor Bahls for review before your board presentation.

04

40% High-Leverage Exam Block

Functions, Polygonal, Transformational, and Modern Geometry exams require independent conceptual mastery. They are open-book but test synthesis over mechanical computation.

05

25% Weekly Discussion Cadence

10 weekly forum discussions (2.5% each) bridge abstract geometry to your creative major (architecture, animation, industrial design, sequential art).

06

Compass & Hand-Drafting Primacy

Euclidean geometry begins in the hands. Compass and straightedge drawing at Stop 1 cannot be automated—spatial intuition begins with tactile construction.

07

Socratic Statement & Reason Rigor

Always format proof drafts with dual columns: Statement and Justification. Isolate your starting given facts from your required target claim before requesting a hint.

08

Stop 4 Board Presentation Defense

You must explain your proof verbally to peer partners at the board ("Bloom"). If you did not reason through the proof yourself, you cannot defend it under peer inquiry.

Reverse Engineering Architecture & Ledger (morluto/rea)

VERIFIED BOUNDARY

This study tool is architected on our reverse-engineering framework (morluto/rea). The system enforces formal runtime invariants to guarantee zero proof leaks, prompt injection resistance, and strict adherence to the SCAD Liberal Arts AI ethics policy.

INV-01 (Precondition) Strict draft length filter: Input must contain $\ge 2$ structured statements; otherwise triggers deterministic refusal.
INV-02 (Leak Prevention) Negative response assertion: Refuses requests for final answers, completed proofs, or direct solution code.
INV-03 (Length Constraint) Output strictly bounded under 120 words to prevent cognitive offloading.
INV-04 (Mandatory Epilogue) Every response unconditionally concludes with the teacher verification disclaimer.
